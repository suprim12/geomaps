# Control Panel Map <Badge type="tip" text="Pro" />

See every entry with a location on one map in the control panel, and jump straight to an entry from its pin. It's the quickest way for editors to spot a store in the wrong place, or to see which areas are covered.

## The Map page

With Geo Maps Pro, **Map** appears in the control panel's sidebar. It shows a section's entries on a map, with nearby pins grouped into numbered bubbles that split apart as you zoom in.

- **Section:** pick any section with a Geo Maps field. If a section has more than one Geo Maps field, pick the field too.
- **Status:** all entries, or only live ones.
- **Search:** narrow the map to entries matching a search, like the entries list.

Click a pin for the entry's title, address and status, with an **Edit** button.

People only see sections they're allowed to view. Entries that only have drawn areas (no pin) appear at the centre of their shapes. Up to 3,000 entries are shown at a time; use search or status to narrow larger sections.

## The Dashboard widget

Add a **Locations Map** widget to the Dashboard for a smaller map of one section, with a link to the full Map page. In the widget's settings, choose the section, the Geo Maps field if there's more than one, and the height.

## Which map

The map follows your [map service](/getting-started/configuration): Google maps with the Google Maps service, and Mapbox or OpenStreetMap tiles otherwise, the same as the field.

## Without Pro

On the free edition, **Map** isn't in the sidebar and the widget isn't offered. A widget added while Pro was active shows a short note instead of the map.
